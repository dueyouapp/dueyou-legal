# DueYou Legal & Support

Public legal and support website for **DueYou: Bills & Renewals**.

Live site: https://dueyouapp.github.io/dueyou-legal/

## Published pages

- `index.html` — site landing page
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy
- `terms.html` — terms of use
- `data-safety.html` — plain-language Google Play Data Safety companion
- `support.html` — support guidance and contact route
- `404.html` — GitHub Pages not-found page
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` — crawler discovery

## Deployment

GitHub Pages publishes the repository's `main` branch from `/` with HTTPS enforced.

## Maintenance rule

The public pages must stay consistent with the actual DueYou app and its current Google Play disclosures. Privacy, Data Safety, payment, analytics, account, cloud-sync, advertising, SDK and support statements must be updated before any app change that makes an existing statement inaccurate.

The legal identity of the publisher/controller must match the entity shown on the applicable app-store listing. Do not guess or substitute a brand name where a legal identity is required.
