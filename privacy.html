Privacy Policy

What DueYou does with data

Plain-English privacy wording for a local-first bills and renewals app.

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Quick answer

DueYou is local-first. Your user-entered bills, renewals, savings notes, goals and reminders stay on your device during normal app use. We do not use ads SDKs, analytics SDKs, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking or a DueYou login in the current app.

1. “Track” means your local records, not tracking you

DueYou is a bills and renewals tracker. That means you can record and organise your own recurring costs in the app. It does not mean that we track you around the internet. No creepy carry-on.

  • No advertising trackers in the current app.
  • No analytics SDK collecting app behaviour in the current app.
  • No bank-link tracking, email scanning, GPS tracking or account profiling.
  • No login or DueYou cloud account in the current app.

2. What you may choose to add

You may choose to add bill, subscription or renewal names; amounts; categories; renewal dates; trial, discount or contract notes; goals; Money Freed records; proof notes; decision notes; and app preferences such as currency, theme, tone and accessibility settings.

This data is stored locally on your device during normal use. It is not sent to us by the current app during normal use.

3. What can leave your device

SituationWhat happens

You email supportWe receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.
You visit these legal pagesGitHub Pages hosts the website and may log technical visitor data such as IP address for security.

DueYou cannot delete or recover copies you exported outside the app. If you send a backup to another service, that copy lives where you sent it.

4. Support emails

If you contact dueyouapp@gmail.com, please do not send passwords, card numbers, full bank statements, private keys or service-account files. Send only what is needed to explain the issue.

We use support emails to reply, investigate bugs, handle privacy requests, manage abuse/security issues and keep basic support records. If you want a support email deleted, contact us with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

5. Internet permission and local-first wording

The Android app may include internet-capable framework or library support. That does not mean your tracked bill data is uploaded to us during normal use. Current DueYou behaviour is local-first: your bill records stay on your device unless you choose to export/share them or contact support.

If future versions add cloud sync, analytics, accounts, live billing or other third-party SDK behaviour, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be reviewed and updated.

6. Payments, Google Play Billing, RevenueCat and Rork

No live paid features are active in the current app. If DueYou Plus or other paid features are added later, purchases will be handled through Google Play Billing and may be managed using RevenueCat or a Rork-provided RevenueCat payment layer.

When live billing is enabled, Google Play and/or RevenueCat may process information needed to provide purchases, restore purchases, manage subscription status, manage entitlements, prevent fraud, comply with law and provide support. This may include product IDs, purchase tokens, transaction identifiers, subscription status, app user IDs or technical device/app information.

We do not receive your full payment card details from Google Play or RevenueCat. Payment card handling is performed by the payment platform.

7. Google Play and GitHub Pages

If you install DueYou from Google Play, Google may process information relating to your download, Google account, device, security checks, reviews, purchases and store activity under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

These public pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or contact forms to this website. GitHub/Microsoft may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs or security information as part of hosting the site.

8. Children

DueYou is not designed for children and is not directed at children. It is intended for adults and household money-admin users.

9. Security and deletion choices

DueYou keeps core bill data local during normal use to reduce exposure. Local device security still matters. If your phone is unlocked, rooted, backed up elsewhere or shared, your local app data may be accessible through those routes.

You can delete app data inside DueYou where the app provides a delete/reset option. You may also uninstall the app or clear app data through Android settings. These actions do not delete exported files, emailed attachments, Google Play records, GitHub Pages visitor logs or copies stored in other services.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Because the core app stores your bill records locally, we usually cannot access or delete those on-device records for you.

For privacy questions about support emails or data you sent to us, email dueyouapp@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when DueYou changes, especially if optional paid features, third-party SDKs, cloud features, accounts, analytics or other new data processing are added. The “Last updated” date will change when the policy changes.
