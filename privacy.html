DueYou: Bills & Renewals

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Plain-English summary: DueYou is designed as a local-first bills, renewals and savings tracker. During normal use, your tracked bill data stays on your device. DueYou does not require a login, does not connect to your bank, does not scan your email, does not use GPS and does not sell your data.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app DueYou: Bills & Renewals, package name com.dueyou.billsrenewals, and this DueYou support website.

For privacy questions, contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com.

2. Data you choose to enter

DueYou works from information you choose to add, confirm or update. This may include:

  • bill, subscription or provider names;
  • categories, prices, currencies, billing cycles and renewal dates;
  • trial, discount, price change or contract notes;
  • check-charge notes and savings confirmation notes;
  • Money Freed records, goals and goal movements;
  • local backup and export files generated by the app.

This information is used to provide DueYou features on your device.

3. Local-first storage

During normal use, DueYou stores your tracked cost data locally on your device. DueYou does not operate a cloud account for your tracked bills, goals, notes, backups or exports.

DueYou does not connect to your bank account and does not read your bank transactions. Any amount, provider, subscription or saving shown in the app depends on information you enter or confirm.

4. Backups and exports

DueYou may let you create backups, readable summaries and redacted exports.

Backups and exports are user-controlled. If you choose to share, email, upload or save an exported file using another app, cloud drive, email provider, messaging app or storage service, that third-party service may process the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Deleting data inside DueYou does not delete backup or export files that you already saved or shared outside the app.

5. Support email

If you contact DueYou by email, your email provider will process the message you send. We will use support messages to respond to your request and keep reasonable support records.

Please do not send bank details, passwords, private keys, service-account JSON files, RevenueCat secret keys, Google Play private credentials or sensitive financial documents.

6. Permissions and third-party services

The current app is designed with no ads, no analytics SDK, no tracking SDK, no GPS/location permission, no camera permission, no microphone permission, no contacts permission, no SMS permission and no notification permission.

The Android app may declare Internet access for technical framework or future billing needs, but DueYou does not upload your tracked bill data during normal use.

DueYou may later add optional in-app purchases or subscriptions through Google Play and RevenueCat. If live billing is added, Google Play and RevenueCat may process purchase-related information needed to handle subscriptions, entitlements, renewals, cancellations, refunds and purchase restoration. DueYou should not send your tracked bills, goals, notes, backups or exported files to RevenueCat.

7. No sale of personal data

DueYou does not sell your personal data.

8. Children

DueYou is not designed for children and should not be marketed as child-directed.

9. Data deletion

You can delete app data on your device using in-app delete controls where available, by clearing app data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

DueYou cannot delete files you already exported, shared, emailed or saved outside the app.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct or delete personal data that we hold about you, such as support emails you send to us. Contact dueyouapp@gmail.com for privacy requests.

Most DueYou app data is stored locally on your own device, so the main way to control it is through the app, Android settings, backups and exports that you manage.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DueYou changes. If there is a material change to privacy behaviour, the updated policy should be made available before or with the relevant app update.

12. Contact

Email: dueyouapp@gmail.com
