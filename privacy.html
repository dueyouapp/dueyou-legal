🔒 Privacy Policy

Your data should stay yours.

DueYou is built as a local-first bills and renewals app. The bill, subscription, renewal, goal and savings information you type into the app is meant to stay on your device during normal use.

In plain English: DueYou helps you track your own bills. DueYou does not track you for ads, follow your behaviour around the internet, sell your data, or build a profile of you. Less fuss, more control.
Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers DueYou: Bills & Renewals, our support/legal website, and any related DueYou app or service that links to this policy.

If we publish other apps later, those apps may link to this policy if the same privacy approach applies. If a future app needs a different data setup, its own page or store listing should say so clearly before users rely on it.

2. What “track” means here

When we say DueYou “tracks bills” or “tracks renewals”, we mean you track your own bills inside the app.

It does not mean we track you, profile you, watch your behaviour for advertising, or sell your information. DueYou has not been built for that carry-on.

3. What stays on your device during normal use

During normal use, the information you enter is stored locally on your device. This can include:

  • bills, subscriptions, renewals, trials and regular costs you add;
  • amounts, dates, categories, notes, review choices and reminder settings;
  • Check Charge notes and Money Freed savings records;
  • goals, category caps, app preferences and local backup/export files you choose to create.

This data is not uploaded to a DueYou cloud account during normal use because DueYou does not currently provide a DueYou cloud account.

4. What we have not added

🚫 No ads

No advertising SDK and no behavioural ad system.

🚫 No analytics tracking

No analytics SDK added to watch how you move through the app.

🚫 No data selling

We do not sell your app data or turn it into a marketing profile.

🚫 No bank link

No bank account, card account or open banking connection.

🚫 No email scanning

DueYou does not read your inbox or scan your receipts.

🚫 No GPS profile

DueYou does not need precise location to do its job.

6. Payment rails if paid features go live

DueYou may offer paid features in the future. If that happens, purchases will be handled through Google Play Billing and payment/entitlement rails used to confirm whether paid access is active.

Those rails may process purchase data needed to make paid features work. This can include purchase status, product ID, purchase token, transaction identifiers, app user identifier, region information needed by the app store, and device/app technical details.

DueYou does not receive your full payment card number from Google Play or the entitlement provider.

If paid features are activated, this policy should be kept aligned with the live app and the providers actually used.

7. Service providers and related apps

To run DueYou properly, we may use trusted service providers for specific jobs. For example: Google Play for app distribution and billing, payment entitlement rails for paid access, GitHub Pages for this website, and email services for support.

These providers are not given your local bill list during normal app use. They only process the information needed for their own job, such as loading this website, delivering support emails, distributing the app, or confirming a purchase.

If future DueYou sister apps link to this policy, the same privacy-first approach applies unless that app clearly says something different. We will not use hidden wording to sneak in data harvesting. That is not the game.

8. Support emails

If you email us, we receive the email and whatever you put in it. That may include your email address, your name if your email account shows it, screenshots, app details, device details and anything else you choose to send.

Please do not send bank statements, full card numbers, passwords, PINs, full account numbers, PPS numbers, ID documents or anything deeply private unless we specifically ask and there is no safer option. A cropped screenshot usually does the job nicely.

9. Exports and backups

DueYou may let you export or back up local app data. Once you choose to send, save or share an export outside the app, the place you send it to is responsible for handling it. That could be your email app, file manager, cloud drive, messaging app or device folder.

Keep exports safe. They may contain bill names, costs, dates, notes and savings records.

10. Children

DueYou is not designed as a child-directed app. It is a money-admin utility for people managing bills, renewals, subscriptions and savings records.

11. Deleting your data

Because normal app data is stored locally, deleting items inside the app, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app may remove your local records from that device. If you created exports or backups, you may also need to delete those wherever you saved them.

If you emailed support and want us to delete that support conversation where reasonably possible, contact dueyouapp@gmail.com.

12. Your rights and control

You control the information you type into DueYou on your device. For support emails or other information we may hold outside the app, you can contact us to ask for access, correction or deletion where the law gives you that right.

We may need to keep some records where required for legal, security, tax, payment or dispute reasons.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, paid features go live, laws change, service providers change, or we find a clearer way to explain something. The updated date will be shown on the page.

Important boundary: DueYou helps you organise your own information. It does not provide financial, legal, insurance, banking, investment, credit, tax or cancellation advice.
