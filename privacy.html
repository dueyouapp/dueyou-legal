Privacy Policy

Privacy without the creepy bits.

DueYou is a local-first bills and renewals app. You enter your own costs, dates, notes and savings records. During normal app use, that tracked app data stays on your device.

Last updated: 24 June 2026 · Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

EL10 summary

You add your own bill and renewal information. DueYou saves it on your phone. We have not added ads, analytics trackers, behavioural tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync. If you export, back up, email support, visit this website, or later use paid features, some data may go to the service you choose or the payment rails needed to process that action.

1. What DueYou is

DueYou: Bills & Renewals helps you organise recurring costs, renewal dates, trials, discounts, category caps, savings notes and goals.

When we say DueYou “tracks bills,” we mean you track your own bills inside the app. We do not mean that we track you around the internet, follow your behaviour for advertising, or build a profile of you.

2. What stays on your device

During normal app use, the following is stored locally on your device:

  • bills, subscriptions, renewals and recurring cost entries
  • amounts, categories, dates, trial and discount details
  • Money Freed records and savings notes
  • goals and goal movements
  • category caps and Smart Save settings
  • app preferences such as theme, region and tone

3. What we have not added

The current app is designed without:

  • advertising SDKs
  • analytics SDKs
  • behavioural tracking tools
  • bank account connection
  • email scanning
  • GPS/location tracking
  • account login
  • cloud sync
  • social sign-in
  • push notification tracking

4. Data that may leave your device

DueYou keeps normal app data local, but some actions can send data outside the app because you choose them or because they are needed for a public website or payment process.

Exports and backups

If you export, back up or share a file, that file may go to the app or service you choose, such as email, cloud storage or messaging. Once you send it there, that service controls how it handles the file.

Support email

If you email support, your email address, message and anything you include are handled by your email provider and ours. Do not send bank passwords, card numbers, full statements or private keys.

This website

These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub/Microsoft may process technical web data such as IP address, browser details and request logs for security, delivery and abuse prevention.

5. Payments and future paid features

The current app setup may show test or planned paid-feature wording, but live paid access is not active unless the app clearly says a real Google Play purchase is being made.

If paid features are enabled later, purchases will be handled through Google Play Billing and payment/entitlement rails such as RevenueCat or an equivalent entitlement provider. These services may process purchase-related data needed to confirm access, such as:

  • product ID or subscription ID
  • purchase token or receipt information
  • purchase status, renewal status and entitlement status
  • an app user ID or anonymous install/user reference
  • basic device, app version and operating system information needed for purchases and support

DueYou does not receive your full payment card number from Google Play or the payment entitlement provider. Refunds, cancellations and subscription management are normally handled through Google Play.

6. App permissions

The app may include internet access for framework, support, store or future payment functions. That does not mean your tracked bill list is uploaded during normal use.

DueYou does not require location, contacts, SMS, calendar, photos, microphone, camera or bank permissions for its core use.

7. Data deletion

You can delete app data on your device using in-app controls where available, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Deleting app data on your device does not delete files you already exported or messages you already sent to support.

8. Children

DueYou is not made for children. It is a general bills and renewals organiser for adults and general users managing household or personal money admin.

9. No sale of app data

We do not sell your tracked bill, renewal, note or savings data. We have not added advertising or behavioural analytics systems to make money from your attention.

10. Security and your part

DueYou is local-first, but your device still matters. Use a screen lock, keep your phone updated, and be careful where you export backups. If you share a backup file with another service, that copy is outside DueYou’s control.

11. Changes to this policy

If DueYou changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated. If paid features, cloud features or new third-party services are added later, the policy and Google Play Data Safety answers must be reviewed before release.
