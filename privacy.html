Privacy Policy

Your data should mind its own business.

DueYou is built to help you organise your own bills and savings notes without turning your private money admin into somebody else’s product.

Local-first No ads No analytics trackers No bank links No selling personal data

Last updated: 25 June 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Quick version

You add your own bill, renewal, trial, savings and goal information. DueYou stores that information on your phone during normal use.

We have not added ads, analytics trackers, behavioural tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync. Useful apps should earn trust by being useful, not by turning your business into somebody else’s.

Some data may leave your phone only when you choose an action that needs it, such as exporting a file, making a backup, emailing support, visiting this website, or using paid features if they are switched on later.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers DueYou: Bills & Renewals, this DueYou legal/support website, and related DueYou apps, pages or services that clearly link to this policy.

If a future DueYou app or feature handles data in a different way, that app or feature should explain the difference clearly. No mystery boxes. No “surprise, we forgot to mention that” carry-on.

2. What you add to DueYou

You may choose to add information such as bill names, subscription names, renewal dates, prices, categories, notes, savings entries, goals and review reminders.

That information is for your own organisation. During normal use, it stays on your device.

Bill and renewal detailsStored on your device so the app can show your costs, dates and reminders.
Savings notes and Money Freed entriesStored on your device so you can record savings you confirm yourself.
Goals and category capsStored on your device so the app can help you organise money-admin decisions.
App settingsStored on your device so DueYou remembers your choices.

3. What DueYou does not do

We want this part to be plain because it matters.

  • We do not connect to your bank account.
  • We do not scan your emails.
  • We do not use GPS tracking.
  • We do not require an account login for the current local-first app.
  • We do not add ads to the current app.
  • We do not add analytics SDKs or behavioural tracking to the current app.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from your bills or savings notes.

When we say DueYou helps you “track bills”, we mean you track your own bills inside the app. We do not mean that DueYou tracks you.

4. When data may leave your device

There are a few normal cases where information may leave your phone because you choose an action or because a service is needed to deliver the app.

Exports and backupsIf you export or back up data, the file may go to the place you choose, such as your email, cloud drive, messages app or file storage.
Support emailsIf you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include.
Website visitsThis legal/support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process normal technical web data such as IP address, browser details and security logs.
App store distributionGoogle Play may process technical, install, crash, review and account information under Google’s own terms and policies.
Future paid featuresIf paid features are enabled later, Google Play Billing and the payment/access rails needed to confirm access may process limited purchase and entitlement information.

5. Payment and access rails

The current legal pages are written to cover the app now and to avoid confusion if paid features are added later.

If paid features are switched on, purchases should be handled through Google Play Billing and payment/access rails used to confirm whether a user has access. These rails may process information such as product ID, purchase token, subscription status, device/app details, country/region needed for the transaction, and a user or install identifier used for entitlement checks.

DueYou should not receive your full card number or full payment card details from those rails. Payment processing is handled by the app store and payment services needed to complete the purchase.

6. Support emails

If you contact support, send only what is needed. A screenshot of a DueYou screen is usually plenty. Please do not send bank statements, card numbers, passwords, government ID, medical information, or anything you would not want sitting in an email inbox.

We may keep support emails long enough to answer the request, fix a problem, protect the app, or keep reasonable records.

7. Children and age

DueYou is designed for general money admin and is not directed at children. Parents or guardians should decide whether a younger person should use it.

8. Your choices

  • You choose what to add to the app.
  • You can edit or delete your local app entries.
  • You choose whether to export or back up your data.
  • You choose whether to contact support.
  • You choose whether to buy paid features if they are offered later.

9. Updates

If DueYou changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy should be updated. The date at the top tells you when this version was last changed.
