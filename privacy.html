Privacy Policy

Your data should mind its own business.

DueYou is built to help you organise your own bills and savings notes without turning your private money admin into somebody else’s product.

Local-first No ads No analytics trackers No bank links No selling personal data

Last updated: 16 August 2026 • Privacy contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Quick version

You add your own bill, renewal, trial, savings and goal information. During normal use, DueYou stores those records on your phone and does not send them to the developer.

The current app does not include ads, analytics trackers, behavioural tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync.

Information can leave your device when you deliberately choose an action that needs another service, such as exporting or backing up a file, emailing support, visiting this website, or using a purchase feature if paid features are enabled.

1. Controller and contact

DueYou is the app and service name. The legal publisher/controller identity is the publisher identity shown for DueYou in the applicable app-store listing. Privacy enquiries and data-protection requests can be sent to dueyouapp@gmail.com.

If DueYou is not yet publicly listed in your region and you need the controller’s legal identity, email the privacy contact before sending personal information.

No Data Protection Officer has been appointed because the current DueYou service does not involve processing that requires one.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers DueYou: Bills & Renewals, this DueYou legal/support website, and related DueYou pages or services that clearly link to this policy.

If a future DueYou feature handles data differently, its privacy information must be updated before or when that feature is introduced.

3. Data you add to the app

You may choose to add information such as bill names, subscription names, renewal dates, prices, categories, notes, savings entries, goals and review reminders.

Bill and renewal detailsStored on your device so the app can show your costs, dates and reminders.
Savings notes and Money Freed entriesStored on your device so you can record savings you confirm yourself.
Goals and category capsStored on your device so the app can help you organise money-admin decisions.
App settingsStored on your device so DueYou remembers your choices.

Because these records remain on your device during normal use, they are not developer-held records simply because you typed them into DueYou.

4. What the current app does not do

  • We do not connect to your bank account.
  • We do not scan your emails.
  • We do not use GPS tracking.
  • We do not require an account login for the current local-first app.
  • We do not add advertising SDKs to the current app.
  • We do not add analytics SDKs or behavioural tracking to the current app.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from your bills or savings notes.
  • We do not make solely automated legal or similarly significant decisions about you.

When DueYou says it helps you “track bills”, it means you track your own bills inside the app. It does not mean DueYou tracks you.

Exports and backupsIf you choose to export, back up, send or store a file, the destination you choose may receive the information contained in that file.
Support emailsIf you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you decide to include.
Website visitsThis website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and normal internet infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser/request information and security logs.
App-store distributionGoogle Play may process account, install, device, crash, review and other store-related information under Google’s own terms and privacy documentation.
Paid featuresIf paid features are enabled, Google Play Billing and any necessary access/entitlement service may process limited purchase and entitlement information needed to complete and verify the purchase.

Where DueYou’s publisher actually processes personal data, the purpose and legal basis depend on why the information is needed.

Support and troubleshootingTo answer requests, investigate problems, protect the service and keep reasonable support records. Processing may be necessary to take steps at your request or support the service you use, and where appropriate may rely on the legitimate interest in operating and securing DueYou.
Website operation and securityTo make the legal/support website available, reliable and secure. The relevant legitimate interest is operating a secure public information and support resource. GitHub also processes information for its own hosting/security purposes under its own documentation.
Purchases and entitlements, if introducedTo process or verify a purchase and provide purchased access. This may be necessary for a contract with you and for applicable legal/accounting obligations. Google Play and other payment providers may also act under their own terms and legal bases.
Legal or security recordsWhere necessary to comply with law, establish or defend legal claims, prevent abuse or protect the service and its users.

DueYou does not rely on consent as a blanket justification for unrelated processing. If a future feature requires consent, that feature should explain the choice and how consent can be withdrawn.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local app records: remain under your control on your device until you edit/delete them, clear the app’s data, remove the app in a way that deletes its local storage, restore/replace the device, or otherwise manage your local files.
  • Exports and backups: are controlled by you and by whichever destination/service you choose. Deleting an entry inside DueYou does not automatically delete a copy that you previously exported elsewhere.
  • Support correspondence: is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, troubleshoot, protect the service, maintain necessary records or deal with a legal claim, and is deleted when it is no longer needed for those purposes.
  • Purchase/legal records: if paid features are introduced, records held by the publisher are retained only for as long as needed for entitlement, accounting, fraud/security, legal or dispute purposes. App stores/payment providers apply their own retention rules to records they control.
  • Hosting/security logs: GitHub and other infrastructure providers apply their own retention periods to information they process as service providers or independent controllers.

8. Security

The current app is designed to minimise developer-held personal data by keeping normal money-admin records local to the device. The legal/support website is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages.

No internet service or email system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers, government ID documents, bank statements or other unnecessary sensitive information. If support needs specific information, send the minimum needed.

9. Service providers and international transfers

Services such as GitHub Pages, Google Play, email providers and future payment/access providers may process information in countries outside Ireland or the European Economic Area.

Those providers are responsible for the transfer mechanisms that apply to processing they carry out under their own terms. Where DueYou’s publisher itself transfers personal data outside the EEA, the transfer must use a lawful GDPR mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards including approved standard contractual clauses where applicable.

10. Your data-protection rights

Where the DueYou publisher holds personal data about you, GDPR rights may include the right to:

  • ask whether your personal data is being processed and request access to it;
  • have inaccurate personal data corrected;
  • request erasure where the legal conditions apply;
  • request restriction of processing where the legal conditions apply;
  • object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive portable data where the right to data portability applies; and
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is specifically based on consent.

To exercise a right, email dueyouapp@gmail.com. We may need enough information to confirm that a request concerns you, but we will not ask for excessive identification.

You also have the right to raise a concern with Ireland’s Data Protection Commission or, where applicable, another competent supervisory authority.

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. A request may be limited where the GDPR or applicable law permits or requires that limitation.

11. Children

DueYou is a general money-admin tool and is not directed at children. The current service does not intentionally build profiles of children or use their data for advertising.

12. Changes to this policy

If DueYou changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy must be updated so that it continues to describe the real app and services. The date at the top shows when this version was last materially updated.
