Privacy Policy

This policy explains what DueYou does and does not do with data. The core principle is simple: your bills, renewals, notes, goals and savings records are stored locally on your device during normal use.

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Important wording: “track” means your local records

DueYou is a bills and renewals tracker. In this context, “track” means that you, the user, can record and organise your own bills, renewals, subscriptions, notes, goals and savings history inside the app.

It does not mean that we track you. DueYou does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, behavioural tracking, bank-link tracking, email scanning, GPS tracking or account-based profiling in the current app.

What data you may choose to add to the app

You may choose to add information such as:

  • bill, subscription or renewal names;
  • amounts, categories, renewal dates and frequencies;
  • trial, discount, contract or price-change notes;
  • goals and goal movement records;
  • Money Freed / confirmed savings records;
  • proof notes or decision notes you enter yourself;
  • app preferences such as theme, tone, currency and accessibility settings.

This information is stored locally on your device during normal use. It is not sent to us by the app during normal use.

What DueYou does not do in the current app

No bank linking, bank login, Open Banking or account aggregation.
No email scanning or inbox access.
No GPS or location tracking.
No advertising SDKs, behavioural advertising or ad tracking.
No analytics SDK collecting app usage behaviour.
No cloud account or DueYou login.
No push notification permission in the current app.

Internet permission and local-first wording

The Android app may include internet-capable libraries or framework support, and the Android manifest may include an Internet permission. The important privacy point is this: the current app does not upload your user-entered bill, renewal, savings, goal or note records to us during normal use.

If this changes in a future version, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before or at the time the new behaviour is released.

Backups, exports and sharing

DueYou may let you create backups, text summaries, redacted summaries or other exports. These actions are user-controlled.

  • If you save an export on your device, it stays where you save it.
  • If you share an export through email, cloud storage, messaging or another app, that destination service may process the file according to its own terms and privacy policy.
  • DueYou cannot delete copies you exported outside the app.

Support emails

If you contact us at dueyouapp@gmail.com, we may receive and process:

  • your email address;
  • your message;
  • device/app details you choose to include;
  • screenshots, backups, exports or other attachments you choose to send.

Please do not send bank statements, card numbers, passwords, API keys, service account files or information you do not want us to receive.

Optional paid features, Google Play Billing and RevenueCat

Current status: DueYou does not currently have live paid features active in the app build being prepared. Some screens may show test/mock purchase wording during development. Mock purchase states are not real purchases.

If optional paid features such as DueYou Plus are added later, purchases will be handled through Google Play Billing and may be managed with RevenueCat or a Rork-provided RevenueCat payment layer.

When live purchases are enabled, Google Play and/or RevenueCat may process information needed to provide purchases, restore purchases, manage subscription status, manage entitlements, prevent fraud, comply with law and provide support. This may include:

  • purchase status, product ID and entitlement status;
  • Google purchase token or transaction-related identifiers;
  • anonymous app user ID or app user ID where configured;
  • device type, operating system and technical purchase-related information;
  • timestamps such as last seen or purchase/update times.

We do not receive your full payment card details from Google Play or RevenueCat. Payment card handling is performed by the payment platform.

RevenueCat’s own policy states that end-user transaction information may include Google purchase tokens, and that optional end-user information may include the user ID used in the app or additional metadata if enabled. RevenueCat also states that it processes end-user information for its customer’s services rather than as the direct customer-facing controller.

Google Play and app store processing

If you install DueYou from Google Play, Google may process information relating to the download, device, account, payments, security checks, reviews and store activity under Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

GitHub Pages website processing

These public legal/support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts to this site. GitHub/Microsoft may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs or security information as part of hosting the website.

Children

DueYou is not designed for children and is not directed at children. It is intended as a general bills and renewals organiser for adults or household money-admin users.

Security

DueYou is designed to minimise data exposure by storing user-entered bill data locally during normal use and avoiding bank links, ads, analytics SDKs and account systems in the current app. Local device security still matters. If your phone is unlocked, rooted, backed up elsewhere or shared with another person, your local app data may be accessible through those routes.

Deleting data

You can delete app data inside DueYou where the app provides a delete/reset option. You may also uninstall the app or clear app data through Android settings. These actions do not delete exported files, emailed attachments or copies stored in other services.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, or complain to a data protection authority. Because the core app stores your bill records locally, we usually cannot access or delete your on-device records for you. Contact us if you have a privacy request about support emails or other data you have sent to us.

Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when DueYou changes, especially if optional paid features, third-party SDKs, cloud features, accounts or other new data processing are added. The “Last updated” date will be changed when the policy is updated.
