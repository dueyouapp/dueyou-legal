Privacy Policy

Privacy without the mystery carry-on.

You add your own bills, renewals, trials, goals, savings notes, and reminders. During normal use, DueYou keeps that app data on your phone. We have not added ads, analytics SDKs, behavioural tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS collection, account login, or cloud sync.

Last updated: 25 June 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Plain-English summary

You track your own costs inside DueYou. DueYou does not track you around the internet. The current app is local-first: your bill and renewal entries stay on your device during normal use. Data may leave your device only when you choose to export, back up, share, email support, visit this website, or use paid features handled by Google Play and payment/access rails.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers DueYou: Bills & Renewals, this DueYou legal/support website, and any future DueYou-branded app, website, support channel, or service that clearly links to this policy.

If a future DueYou app uses different data, permissions, cloud features, sign-in, or paid features, we should update the relevant policy, store listing, and Data Safety information before release. No quiet switcheroo.

2. What DueYou is

DueYou is a bills, renewals, subscriptions, trials, reminders, goals, and savings tracker. That means you track your own information in the app so your money-admin is easier to see.

When we say DueYou “tracks bills,” we do not mean we track your behaviour for ads, analytics, profiling, or data selling. We mean the app gives you a private place to write down and review what is due.

3. What stays on your phone during normal use

In the current build, the following information is stored locally on your device during normal app use:

  • Bills, subscriptions, renewals, trials, and regular costs you add.
  • Amounts, dates, categories, notes, review reminders, and Check Charge results you enter.
  • Money Freed records, goal names, target amounts, category caps, and savings notes you create.
  • App settings, local backups, and exports you choose to create.

We do not run a DueYou cloud account for this app data. We do not sign you in. We do not read your bank. We do not scan your email. We do not ask for GPS. We do not sell this data. Nice and boring, the good kind of boring.

4. What we have not added

For the current app, we have not added:

  • Ads or ad networks.
  • Analytics SDKs for measuring your behaviour.
  • Behavioural tracking, advertising profiles, or data broker tools.
  • Bank linking, open banking, card scraping, or account aggregation.
  • Email scanning, inbox reading, SMS reading, or contact-list access.
  • GPS location collection.
  • Developer cloud sync for your tracked bill data.
  • Account login with DueYou.

Our product stance is simple: useful apps should earn money by being useful, not by quietly turning people into data mines.

6. Exports, backups, and sharing

DueYou may let you create a local backup or export. That is useful, but it also means you need to choose the destination carefully.

If you export to your files, email, cloud drive, messenger, notes app, or another service, that destination may process the file under its own privacy rules. DueYou cannot control what another app or service does after you send data there.

Practical rule: only export to places you trust. Keep the “I’ll just send this anywhere” energy for memes, not money records.

7. Support emails

If you contact us at dueyouapp@gmail.com, we may receive your email address, message, screenshots, and any file or detail you include.

Please do not send bank passwords, full card numbers, national ID numbers, medical information, or anything you would not want sitting in an email inbox. If you need to show an issue, blank out sensitive details first.

We use support emails to reply, investigate bugs, improve the app, protect the service, and keep records where needed.

8. Payments and paid features

DueYou may offer paid features in the future. If that happens, payments should be handled through Google Play Billing and payment/access rails that confirm whether paid features should be unlocked.

Those services may process payment-related and technical information such as:

  • Product or subscription ID.
  • Purchase token, order reference, or transaction status.
  • Entitlement status, such as whether Plus is active.
  • App version, device type, operating system, and technical data needed for purchase checks.

DueYou does not receive your full payment card details from Google Play. Refunds, payment methods, taxes, and billing disputes may be handled through Google Play according to Google Play’s rules.

If paid features are added, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety information should be checked again before release. We will not bury a payment-data change in the long grass.

9. Website hosting

This legal/support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages is a static website hosting service. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub says the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed into GitHub.

This website does not need cookies, ads, tracking pixels, or JavaScript to work.

10. What we use data for

Where data reaches us or our service providers, we use it only for sensible reasons:

  • Running and improving DueYou.
  • Answering support requests.
  • Fixing bugs and checking reported problems.
  • Managing purchases and paid-feature access, if paid features are enabled.
  • Security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and keeping records we are required to keep.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share user-entered bill data with advertisers or data brokers. We do not build advertising profiles.

11. Related DueYou apps and services

We may create other DueYou-branded apps, pages, tools, or services. If they link to this policy, this policy applies unless the other app or page says something different.

If a future app uses cloud sync, login, AI, device permissions, analytics, ads, or a different data setup, that app needs its own clear wording before users rely on it. Future-proof does not mean vague. It means we keep the policy honest as the family grows.

12. Data deletion

Because the current app keeps your tracked bill data on your device during normal use, you can delete your local app data from inside the app where tools are provided, or by deleting the app data from your Android settings.

If you sent us a support email and want us to delete support correspondence where we reasonably can, email dueyouapp@gmail.com. We may keep limited records where needed for legal, security, billing, fraud-prevention, or business record reasons.

13. Security

No app or website can promise perfect security. DueYou reduces risk by avoiding unnecessary collection in the first place. Less collected means less to lose. Radical idea, apparently.

You can help by keeping your phone locked, keeping Android updated, using trusted backup locations, and not emailing sensitive financial details unless they are needed and safely redacted.

14. Children

DueYou is not designed for children. It is a money-admin app for people managing bills, renewals, subscriptions, trials, and savings notes. If a child uses the app, a parent or guardian should supervise that use.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DueYou improves, laws change, store rules change, or new features are added.

If a change is important, we should make it clear in the policy, app, store listing, or support page as appropriate. No “surprise, you should have guessed” nonsense.

Bottom line

DueYou is here to help people get a better grip on what is due, what can be checked, and what they have saved. Privacy is part of the value, not a decoration slapped on at the end.

DueYou helps people keep their own bills and renewals visible. No ads. No behavioural tracking. No selling personal data.
