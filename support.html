🛟 Support

Need a hand? Send the useful bits, not your life story.

For help with DueYou, email us with a clear description of the issue. Cropped screenshots are welcome. Full bank statements are not needed and should stay safely with you.

Email supportRead Privacy

Contact

Email: dueyouapp@gmail.com

What to include

  • what you were trying to do;
  • what happened instead;
  • your Android device model if you know it;
  • your app version if visible;
  • a cropped screenshot if it helps.

What not to send

Please do not send full bank statements, card numbers, passwords, PINs, PPS numbers, ID documents, full account numbers or anything deeply private.

If a screenshot shows private details, crop or blur it first. Keep the sensitive stuff under your own roof.

Support boundaries

We can help with app issues, bugs, setup questions and feedback. We cannot give financial, legal, insurance, banking, tax, cancellation or provider-specific advice.

Feature ideas

Good ideas are welcome. DueYou is built to be useful, not noisy. If a feature helps people save time, reduce stress or make clearer decisions without harvesting data, it is the kind of thing worth considering.

Related apps

If we create more apps, this support address may also be used for related DueYou apps unless that app gives a different support contact.
