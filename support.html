Support

Need a hand? Grand, start here.

Support for DueYou is handled by email. Send what helps us understand the issue, and keep sensitive private details out of it unless truly needed.

Email support No passwords No card numbers Useful screenshots Clear details

Last updated: 25 June 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Quick version

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com if you need help with DueYou. Keep it practical: what happened, what device you used, and what you expected to happen.

Please do not send sensitive financial documents, passwords, full card numbers, government ID, or private details unless we specifically ask for something limited. Support should fix the app, not collect your life story.

What to include

  • Your device model and Android version if you know them.
  • The DueYou screen or feature involved.
  • What you tapped.
  • What you expected.
  • What actually happened.
  • A screenshot of the DueYou screen if useful.

What not to include

  • Full bank statements.
  • Full card numbers.
  • Passwords or one-time codes.
  • Government ID numbers.
  • Medical information.
  • Anything you would not want stored in an email inbox.

Response time

DueYou is a small app, so support is handled carefully rather than like a giant call centre with hold music from 2008. We aim to reply as soon as reasonably possible.

Privacy and support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use that to answer you, troubleshoot issues, improve the app, and keep reasonable records.

For more detail, see the Privacy Policy.
