Support

Need a hand? Start here.

For DueYou support, email dueyouapp@gmail.com. Keep it clear, and please do not send sensitive financial details.

What to include

  • your device model, if you know it
  • Android version, if you know it
  • what you tapped
  • what you expected
  • what happened instead
  • a screenshot if it does not show private information

What not to send

  • bank login details
  • full card numbers
  • full bank statements
  • private keys or passwords
  • documents you would not want in an email

Privacy questions

If you have a privacy question, use the subject line Privacy question. We will try to respond as clearly as possible.

Delete app data

You can delete local app data using the app’s controls where available, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling the app. This does not delete exported files or emails you already sent.

Payments and refunds

If paid features are enabled later, purchases, cancellations and refunds are normally managed through Google Play. DueYou support can help explain where to look, but Google Play controls the payment process.

Response times

This is a small independent app project. We will do our best to help, but support may not be instant. No call centre. No script-reading robot pretending to care. Just a real attempt to fix useful things.

Not for emergencies

DueYou support is not financial, legal, banking, insurance, debt or emergency support. If something is urgent, contact the relevant provider, bank, regulator, emergency service or qualified professional.
