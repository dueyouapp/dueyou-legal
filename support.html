Support

Need help? Send the useful bits, keep the private bits private.

Email support at dueyouapp@gmail.com. We will do our best to help with app issues, store questions, privacy questions, and clear bug reports.

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Best support email format

Tell us what happened, what you expected, what phone you use, and whether you can repeat the issue. Screenshots help, but please blank out private financial details first.

What to include

  • Your phone model and Android version, if you know them.
  • The DueYou app version, if shown in the app.
  • The screen or feature where the issue happened.
  • The steps that caused the issue.
  • A screenshot if useful, with private details covered.

What not to send

  • Bank passwords or login details.
  • Full card numbers.
  • National ID numbers.
  • Private medical information.
  • Full statements unless specifically needed and safely redacted.

Support is easier when the message is clear. It is also safer when the sensitive bits stay out of email.

What we can help with

  • Bug reports.
  • Privacy or Data Safety questions.
  • Store listing or payment questions.
  • Problems with app features.
  • Feedback that would make DueYou more useful.

What we cannot do

We cannot contact providers for you, cancel services for you, give financial advice, give legal advice, guarantee savings, or decide whether a provider’s terms allow a particular action.

We can help you use DueYou. We cannot become your broadband provider’s complaints department, tempting as that may be some days.
