Support

Need help with DueYou? Use the support email below. Please do not send sensitive bank statements, card details, passwords or private keys.

Email support

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com

Include your device model, Android version, what you were trying to do, and screenshots only if you are comfortable sharing them.

Privacy request

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line if your question is about support emails or data you sent to us.

Common questions

Does DueYou connect to my bank?

No. DueYou does not connect to bank accounts and does not ask for banking login details.

Where is my tracked bill data stored?

Your user-entered bill, renewal, savings, goal and note records are stored locally on your device during normal use.

Can DueYou delete exported files for me?

No. If you export or share a file outside the app, you control that copy through the destination service you chose.

Does DueYou offer financial advice?

No. DueYou is a tracker, organiser and reminder tool. It does not provide financial, legal, insurance, banking, investment, credit or cancellation advice.

What if paid features are added later?

Paid features would be handled through Google Play Billing and may use RevenueCat or a Rork payment layer to manage purchase status and entitlements. We will update the policy and app wording when live paid features are enabled.

Before contacting support

  • Check that you are using the latest version from Google Play.
  • Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, bank statements or service account files.
  • If reporting a bug, explain the exact screen and taps that caused it.
  • If asking about privacy, say whether your question relates to the app, support email, GitHub Pages website or future paid features.
