Support

Help without the headache

How to contact DueYou support and what not to send.

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Need help?

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com. We try to keep support calm, clear and useful. No need to send the whole kitchen sink.

Email support

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com

Include your device model, Android version, app version if visible, what you were trying to do, and the exact taps that led to the issue.

Please do not send

  • Passwords or private keys.
  • Full bank statements.
  • Payment card numbers.
  • Google service account JSON files.
  • Anything you would not want sitting in an email inbox.

Privacy requests

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line if your question is about support emails or data you sent to us.

DueYou usually cannot access or delete your on-device bill records because those are stored locally on your phone during normal app use. You can delete app data in DueYou where available, uninstall the app, or clear app data in Android settings.

Billing help

No live paid features are active in the current app. If paid features are added later, purchases will be handled by Google Play Billing and may use RevenueCat or a Rork-provided RevenueCat payment layer.

For future subscription cancellation or refund routes, Google Play’s own purchase controls and policies may apply. DueYou support can help explain where to look, but cannot directly manage your Google Play account.

Common questions

Does DueYou connect to my bank?

No. DueYou does not ask for banking login details and does not connect to bank accounts.

Where is my tracked bill data stored?

Your user-entered bill, renewal, savings, goal and note records are stored locally on your device during normal use.

Does DueYou track me?

No ads SDK. No analytics SDK. No behavioural ad tracking in the current app. “Track bills” means you track your own costs locally.

Can DueYou delete exported files for me?

No. If you export or share a file outside the app, you control that copy through the destination service you chose.

Does DueYou offer financial advice?

No. DueYou is a tracker, organiser and reminder tool. It does not provide financial, legal, insurance, banking, investment, credit or cancellation advice.
