Support

Need a hand? Start here.

Support for DueYou is handled by email. Send what helps us understand the issue and keep sensitive private details out of it unless they are genuinely necessary.

Last updated: 16 August 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Quick version

Email dueyouapp@gmail.com if you need help with DueYou. Keep it practical: what happened, the screen or feature involved, what you expected and what actually happened.

Please do not send sensitive financial documents, passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers, government ID or unrelated private information. Support should receive the minimum information needed to solve the problem.

What to include

  • Your device model and Android version, if relevant and known.
  • The DueYou screen or feature involved.
  • The steps you took immediately before the problem.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened, including the exact error text if one appeared.
  • A screenshot of the DueYou screen if it helps and does not expose unrelated sensitive information.

What not to include

  • Passwords or one-time authentication codes.
  • Full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Full bank statements.
  • Government ID numbers or identity documents.
  • Medical information.
  • Other people’s personal information unless it is strictly necessary.
  • Anything unrelated to the support problem.

Privacy and data-protection requests

The same address, dueyouapp@gmail.com, can be used for privacy questions or to exercise an applicable data-protection right concerning personal data held by the DueYou publisher.

Use a clear subject such as Privacy request and explain what you are asking for. We may ask for limited information needed to make sure personal data is not disclosed or changed for the wrong person.

Remember that normal DueYou bill, renewal, savings and goal records are designed to stay on your own device. If the publisher does not hold a copy, there is no server-side copy for support to retrieve or delete.

Response handling

Support is handled directly rather than through a large call centre. We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible. Formal data-protection requests are handled within the time limits that apply under data-protection law.

Support correspondence is retained only while reasonably needed for support, troubleshooting, security, necessary records or legal claims, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Security reports

If you believe you have found a security or privacy weakness in DueYou, email the support address with a concise description and enough information to reproduce the issue safely. Do not exploit the issue against other people, access data you do not own or publish sensitive details before there has been a reasonable opportunity to investigate.
