🛡️ Data Safety

The tidy map of what happens to data.

This page explains DueYou’s data setup in simple terms. It is meant to match the privacy policy and help users understand what the app does, without needing three coffees and a law degree.

Current core: no bank links, no ads, no analytics SDK, no behaviour tracking, no email scanning, no GPS profile, and no DueYou cloud account for your tracked bill data.

1. Current app data map

2. Data we do not use for ads

DueYou does not use your local bill list, renewal dates, savings notes or goals for behavioural advertising. We do not sell that information. We do not build a profile of your spending habits for marketers.

The app should help you mind your own business, not turn your business into somebody else’s.

3. Local data is not the same as collected data

Information that stays on your device during normal use is handled locally. It is not the same as DueYou collecting it on a server.

If you choose to export, back up, share a file, email support, visit this website, or use future paid features, then those specific actions may involve data leaving your device as explained in the Privacy Policy.

4. Payment and entitlement data if paid features go live

If paid features are enabled, payment access may be checked through Google Play Billing and payment entitlement rails. Those systems may process purchase status, product IDs, purchase tokens, transaction identifiers, app/device technical details and an app user identifier.

That does not mean DueYou receives your full payment card number. Payment card handling is managed by the app store/payment systems.

5. Future DueYou apps and related services

If we create more apps, each app should explain its own data setup. If a related app links to this Data Safety page, the same privacy-first approach applies unless that app clearly says otherwise.

The rule is simple: if data leaves the device for an app feature, payment, support, website hosting, legal requirement or user export, it should be explained clearly.

6. Safety promise

DueYou’s direction: earn trust through useful features, not through ads, behavioural tracking or data harvesting.
