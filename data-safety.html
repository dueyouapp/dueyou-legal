Data Safety Summary

This page is a plain-English companion to the Privacy Policy. It helps users and reviewers understand the current app’s data behaviour.

Current app status

The current DueYou app is local-first. User-entered bill, subscription, renewal, goal, note and savings records are stored locally on the device during normal use and are not uploaded to us by the app during normal use.

Current “No” areas

  • No bank account connection.
  • No email scanning.
  • No GPS/location collection.
  • No DueYou login/account.
  • No advertising SDK.
  • No analytics SDK collecting app behaviour.
  • No live paid subscription processing in the current build.

User-controlled sharing

DueYou may allow the user to create exports or backups. If the user chooses to share those files with another app or service, that action is user-controlled and the selected destination may process the file.

Support communications

If a user emails support, the support inbox receives the user’s email address, message and any attachments the user chooses to send.

Future paid features

If DueYou Plus or other paid features are enabled later, Google Play Billing and/or RevenueCat may process purchase and entitlement information. The Privacy Policy should be reviewed and the Google Play Data Safety form should be updated before release of live paid billing.

Reviewer note

Google Play Data Safety declarations should be kept consistent with the actual app build, permissions, SDKs and privacy policy. If RevenueCat, Google Play Billing, analytics, cloud sync, login or any other SDK/service is added later, this summary and the Play Console declaration must be reviewed again.
