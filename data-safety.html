Data Safety Summary

What happens to your data, without the fog machine.

This page explains DueYou’s data behaviour in plain English for users and Google Play setup.

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Current app summary

During normal app use, DueYou stores tracked bills, renewals, notes, goals and savings records locally on your device. We have not added advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, behavioural trackers, account login, bank linking, email scanning, GPS tracking or cloud sync.

Data collected by DueYou during normal app use

The current app is designed so normal bill-tracking data is processed on your device and not sent to DueYou’s servers.

Data shared by user action

If you export, back up, share a file or email support, you are choosing to send information to another app or service.

Payment data if paid features are enabled

If DueYou later enables paid features, payment rails may need to process purchase tokens, product IDs, entitlement state, app user IDs or similar technical purchase records. This is used to confirm whether paid features should be unlocked. It is not used for advertising.

Important wording

“DueYou does not track users for ads or analytics” does not mean “nothing is ever stored.” The app stores the bill and renewal information you choose to add on your own device. That is the whole point of the app.
