Data Safety

A simple map of what stays local and what may leave.

This page explains DueYou’s data setup in plain terms, especially for Google Play Data Safety and anyone who wants the no-nonsense version.

Local records User-controlled exports Support by email Website hosting Future payment rails

Last updated: 25 June 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Quick version

DueYou is local-first. The bill, renewal, trial, savings and goal records you add are stored on your device during normal use.

We have not added ads, analytics trackers, behavioural tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync. If you choose exports, backups, support emails, website visits or future paid features, data may go to the service needed for that action.

Data map

User-added bill and renewal recordsStored locally on your device during normal use.
Savings notes and goalsStored locally on your device during normal use.
Exports and backupsOnly leave the device if you choose to export, back up, send or store them elsewhere.
Support emailsSent to us only if you choose to contact support.
Website visitsHandled by GitHub Pages and normal browser/hosting systems.
Future paid featuresIf enabled, purchase and access data may be handled by Google Play Billing and payment/access rails.

What is not in the current app

  • No ads SDK.
  • No analytics SDK.
  • No behaviour tracking.
  • No bank connection.
  • No email scanning.
  • No GPS tracking.
  • No account login.
  • No cloud sync.
  • No selling personal data.

How to read this with Google Play Data Safety

Local-only app records are different from data collected by a developer. If information stays on the user’s device during normal use, it is not the same as us collecting it from the device.

Separate actions, such as support emails, exports, website visits and payment/access rails, are explained separately because they may involve other services.

Best rule of thumb

If you type it into DueYou for your own use, it normally stays with you. If you choose to send, export, back up, email, buy or visit, the service needed for that choice may receive what it needs to complete that choice.
