Data Safety

A simple map of what stays local and what may leave.

This page explains DueYou’s current data setup in plain terms. The full legal detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Local records User-controlled exports Support by email Website hosting No ad tracking

Last updated: 16 August 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Quick version

DueYou is local-first. The bill, renewal, trial, savings and goal records you add are stored on your device during normal use.

The current app does not include ads, analytics trackers, behavioural tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync.

If you deliberately export or back up a file, email support, visit this website, or use a future paid feature, the service needed for that action may receive the information required to perform it.

Current data map

User-added bill and renewal recordsStored locally on your device during normal use. They are not automatically sent to the developer.
Savings notes and goalsStored locally on your device during normal use.
App settingsStored locally so the app remembers your choices.
Exports and backupsLeave the device only when you choose to export, back up, send or store them elsewhere. The destination you choose then controls its copy.
Support emailsSent to the support inbox only if you choose to contact support. We receive your email address and whatever you include.
Website visitsDelivered through GitHub Pages and normal internet infrastructure, which may process technical request and security information.
App-store activityGoogle Play may process store/account/device information under Google’s own policies.
Future paid featuresIf enabled, Google Play Billing and any necessary entitlement service may process limited purchase/access information needed to provide paid access.

What is not in the current app

  • No advertising SDK.
  • No analytics SDK.
  • No behavioural advertising or profiling.
  • No bank connection.
  • No email scanning.
  • No GPS tracking.
  • No account login.
  • No cloud sync.
  • No sale of personal data.

Retention and deletion

  • Local app records remain under your control on the device until you edit/delete them or otherwise remove the app’s local data.
  • Deleting something inside DueYou does not delete a separate copy you previously exported to another service or location.
  • Support correspondence is kept only while reasonably needed for support, troubleshooting, security, necessary records or legal claims.
  • If purchase features are introduced, publisher-held purchase/entitlement records are kept only as long as required for access, accounting, security, disputes or legal obligations.
  • GitHub, Google and other services apply their own retention rules to data they independently process.

How this relates to Google Play Data Safety

Google Play distinguishes between information that stays only on a user’s device and information collected or shared off-device. DueYou’s normal local records are designed to stay local.

The Play Console Data Safety declaration must always match the real app version currently distributed, including any third-party SDKs or new features. If DueYou later adds analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud sync, new permissions, payment infrastructure or any other off-device processing, both the Play declaration and these public pages must be reviewed before release.

Your privacy choices

You decide what to enter, edit and delete locally, whether to export or back up data, whether to contact support, and whether to use any paid feature if one is offered.

Where the DueYou publisher holds personal data about you, applicable GDPR rights are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Best rule of thumb

If you type something into DueYou for your own use, it normally stays with you. If you choose to send, export, back up, email, buy or visit, the service needed for that choice may receive what it needs to complete that choice.
