Current build summary

The current DueYou app is local-first. User-entered bill, subscription, renewal, goal, note and savings records are stored locally on the device during normal app use and are not uploaded to us by the current app during normal use.

Current “No” areas

No bank connectionNo email scanningNo GPS/location collectionNo DueYou loginNo ads SDKNo analytics SDKNo live paid billing

Google Play Data Safety reminder

This page is a plain-English companion to the Privacy Policy. The Google Play Console form must still match the actual app build, manifest permissions, SDKs, app behaviour, privacy policy and any third-party libraries.

If RevenueCat, Google Play Billing, analytics, cloud sync, login, crash reporting, push notifications or any other SDK/service is added later, this page and the Play Console declaration must be reviewed before release.

Current declaration direction

For the current local-first build, the app should be described as not collecting user-entered bill data off-device during normal use. Be careful not to confuse local user records with data collection. Google defines collection around transmitting data off the user’s device, while purely on-device processing is treated differently in the Data Safety guidance.

Do not submit the Data Safety form until the final build, permissions and SDKs are checked again. Boring and accurate beats brave and wrong.
