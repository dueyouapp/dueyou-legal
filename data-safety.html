Data Safety Summary

What leaves your phone, what does not, and why.

This page is written to match DueYou’s local-first design and help explain the Google Play Data Safety answers in normal human language.

Last updated: 25 June 2026 • Contact: dueyouapp@gmail.com

Plain-English summary

Current app use is local-first. Your tracked bills, renewals, goals, and savings notes stay on your phone during normal use. We have not added ads, analytics SDKs, behavioural tracking, bank links, account login, cloud sync, email scanning, GPS collection, or data-selling tools.

2. How this lines up with Google Play wording

Google Play treats data as “collected” when it is transmitted off a user’s device. Google also says local-only processing on the user’s device does not need to be declared as collected.

That is why local bill entries are different from support emails, website visits, exports, or payment checks. Local entries stay on your phone during normal use. The other actions may involve another service because you chose to send something, visit a site, or use a paid feature.

3. Data we do not collect for the current app

  • Location data.
  • Contacts.
  • Photos or videos.
  • Audio recordings.
  • Emails or SMS.
  • Bank account logins.
  • Credit card numbers.
  • Advertising ID for ad tracking.
  • Behaviour profiles.
  • App analytics about what buttons you press.

4. User-controlled sharing

If you export, back up, share, or email information, that action may send data outside the app. That is user-controlled sharing. Choose the destination carefully and blank out sensitive information before contacting support.

5. Future payment/access rails

If DueYou adds paid features, Google Play Billing and payment/access rails may process the minimum purchase and entitlement data needed to confirm payment and unlock paid features. That may include product IDs, purchase tokens, subscription status, entitlement status, device/app version, and similar technical purchase-check information.

DueYou does not receive full card details from Google Play. If paid features go live, the Play Data Safety form should be checked again before release.

6. Service providers

Some services may be needed to publish, support, host, or take payment for DueYou. Examples include Google Play, website hosting, email support, and payment/access rails. These providers should only be used for the job they are needed for, not to build advertising profiles from your bill records.

Privacy principle

If DueYou does not need a piece of data to help you, it should not ask for it. Less noise, less risk, less nonsense.

DueYou helps people keep their own bills and renewals visible. No ads. No behavioural tracking. No selling personal data.
