EL10 summary

Use DueYou to organise your own money admin. Do not use it to break provider rules, fake eligibility, dodge payments, commit fraud, or treat app suggestions as financial or legal advice. Keep your own records and check provider terms before changing a service.

1. What DueYou does

DueYou is a local-first tracker and organiser for recurring costs. It can help you record bills, trials, renewals, discounts, savings decisions, category caps and goals.

DueYou gives organisation and decision-support only. It does not make decisions for you.

2. What DueYou is not

  • not a bank

  • not a lender or credit provider
  • not an insurer or broker
  • not an accountant
  • not a financial adviser
  • not a legal adviser
  • not a cancellation agent

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for checking:

  • your provider’s terms
  • renewal dates and cancellation deadlines
  • whether a discount, trial or offer still applies
  • whether a change is right for you
  • the accuracy of information you enter

4. Smart saving boundaries

DueYou may suggest practical ideas such as reviewing a subscription, using an official cheaper plan, pausing a service where allowed, checking publicly available offers, or comparing official provider options.

Do not use DueYou to do anything illegal, misleading or against provider terms. That includes:

  • piracy or illegal streaming
  • fake accounts or fake trials
  • fake student, employee or family eligibility
  • coupon fraud or fake referral activity
  • chargeback abuse
  • insurance misrepresentation
  • using card replacement as a substitute for cancelling a contract
  • sharing accounts outside provider terms
  • bypassing paywalls or access controls

Keep it clean: use official routes, published offers and provider-approved options. You’ll sleep better, and so will we.

5. Accuracy

DueYou relies on the information you add. It may estimate savings, renewals or useful actions, but those estimates may be wrong if dates, amounts or notes are wrong or outdated.

6. Money Freed

Money Freed is a record-keeping feature. It helps you record confirmed savings and savings decisions. It is not a guarantee that money has been saved, recovered or transferred.

7. Backups and exports

If you export or back up your data, keep the file safe. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read what you exported unless you protect it.

8. Support

Support is provided by email where possible. Do not send passwords, full card numbers, private keys, bank login details or full statements.

Local test toggles, backups or screenshots are not proof of purchase. Real paid access must come from the active payment and entitlement system.

DueYou is designed to help you spot and record opportunities. It does not promise that you will save money.
