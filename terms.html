Terms of Use

The rules, boundaries and clean-saving promise

DueYou helps with organisation, not financial/legal advice or provider rule-bending.

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Short version

Use DueYou to organise your own bills, renewals, savings notes and reminders. Do not use it for fraud, piracy, fake eligibility, payment dodging or terms-breaking tricks. DueYou helps with admin; it does not make decisions for you.

1. What DueYou is

DueYou is a local bills and renewals organiser. It helps users record recurring costs, renewal dates, trials, discounts, savings notes, reminders, goals and local summaries.

It does not connect to your bank, make payments, cancel services for you, negotiate with providers, guarantee savings or provide regulated services.

2. No financial, legal, insurance, banking or cancellation advice

DueYou provides organisation and decision-support tools only. It does not provide financial advice, legal advice, insurance advice, banking services, lending, credit services, investment advice, debt advice, tax advice, regulated cancellation services or provider representation.

You remain responsible for checking provider terms, renewal terms, cancellation rules, contract dates, eligibility, local laws and your own circumstances before changing, pausing, cancelling, downgrading, switching or paying for any service.

3. Your records are your responsibility

You are responsible for the information you add to DueYou and for checking whether it is correct. DueYou may calculate summaries from what you enter, but those summaries depend on your entries being accurate and up to date.

DueYou is not a replacement for bank statements, provider bills, contracts, official renewal notices, tax records or professional advice.

4. Backups, exports and local data

DueYou may provide backup and export features. You decide where to save or share exported files. If you send a backup or export to another app, email account or cloud service, that destination may process the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

DueYou cannot recover or delete files after you export them outside the app. Keep backups safe and avoid sharing sensitive information unless you mean to.

5. Smart saving, clean routes only

DueYou may suggest practical review prompts such as checking a trial, reviewing a price rise, pausing a service, comparing official plans, using provider-published offers or considering provider-approved options.

Keep it clean: use official routes, published offers and provider-approved options. No dodgy business, no “sure it’ll be grand” loopholes that would get anyone in trouble.

  • No piracy or illegal streaming.
  • No fake accounts or fake trials.
  • No false student, employee or family eligibility.
  • No chargeback abuse, coupon fraud or payment avoidance.
  • No account sharing outside provider terms.
  • No misrepresentation to insurers, lenders, providers or support teams.

6. Optional paid features

DueYou may later offer optional paid features such as DueYou Plus. If paid features are enabled, purchases and subscriptions will be processed through Google Play Billing and may be managed with RevenueCat or a Rork-provided RevenueCat payment layer.

Any paid feature terms, subscription periods, prices, renewals, cancellation routes and refund rules shown in Google Play will apply. Do not treat any in-app test or mock purchase wording as a real purchase until live billing is enabled through Google Play.

7. Availability, changes and errors

We aim to keep DueYou useful and reliable, but no app is perfect. Features may change, be improved, be removed, be delayed, or work differently across devices. You should keep your own records for important bills and contracts.

8. Acceptable use

Do not misuse DueYou, attempt to break it, reverse-engineer paid entitlements, bypass payment systems, upload malicious content, misuse support, or use the app in a way that is unlawful or harmful.

9. Limits of responsibility

DueYou is provided as a practical organiser. To the fullest extent allowed by law, we are not responsible for losses caused by inaccurate user entries, missed provider notices, contract terms, price changes, failed cancellations, decisions you make, exported files you share, or services outside DueYou’s control.

Nothing in these terms removes consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

For support, email dueyouapp@gmail.com. Please include only the information needed to understand your question.
