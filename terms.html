Quick version

Use DueYou to organise your own bills, renewals, trials, goals and savings notes. Do not use it for fraud, fake eligibility, piracy, chargeback abuse, provider-term dodging, or anything illegal.

DueYou helps with organisation and decisions. It does not make those decisions for you, cancel services for you, give financial advice, or promise savings. It is a good helper, not a magic wand with a standing order.

You may use DueYou for personal money admin, household organisation, bill review, renewal reminders, savings notes and related planning.

You are responsible for checking your own bills, provider terms, renewal dates, prices and decisions. Providers change their terms and prices. Life is lively like that.

2. What DueYou is not

DueYou is not a bank, accountant, tax adviser, financial adviser, legal adviser, insurer, lender, broker, switching service, cancellation agent, payment provider or debt service.

Any information in the app is general organisation support only. You should use your own judgement and get professional advice where needed.

3. No illegal or unfair use

DueYou should help people save money cleanly. Good savings should feel clever, not like you are waiting for an awkward email from a provider.

  • Do not use DueYou to plan fraud or deception.
  • Do not fake eligibility for discounts, refunds or offers.
  • Do not use it for piracy or illegal streaming.
  • Do not use it to dodge valid provider terms.
  • Do not use it for chargeback abuse or payment dodging.

Reminders are helpful nudges, not legal notices. Do not rely on DueYou as your only warning for important renewals, cancellations, payments, insurance, tax, legal deadlines or anything high-risk.
