Private by default

Normal app records stay on your phone. The current app has no ads, analytics trackers, behaviour tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync.

Clear boundaries

DueYou is an organiser and decision-support app. It is not a bank, financial adviser, broker, cancellation agent, insurer or lender.

Quick version

You use DueYou to track information about your own bills and renewals. That does not mean DueYou tracks you around the internet, builds an advertising profile from your records or sells your personal data.

If you deliberately export or back up a file, email support, visit this website, or use a future purchase feature, the service needed for that action may receive the information required to complete it. Those cases are explained in the Privacy Policy and Data Safety page.
