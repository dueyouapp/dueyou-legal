Plain-English summary

DueYou does not track users for advertising. Users may use DueYou to track their own bills and renewals locally. That local bill data is not sent to us during normal use. If users choose to export, back up, email support, or later buy optional Plus features through Google Play, other services may process data needed for that user-chosen action.
