Helpful by design

DueYou helps you keep bills, renewals, trials, subscriptions, savings notes and goals tidy. Less scrambling, more knowing what is due.

🔒

Private by default

Your normal app records stay on your phone. We have not added ads, analytics trackers, behaviour tracking, bank links, email scanning, GPS tracking, account login or cloud sync.

🧭

Clear boundaries

DueYou is an organiser and decision-support app. It is not a bank, adviser, broker, cancellation agent, insurer or lender. No pretending, no fog machine.

Quick version

You use DueYou to track your own bills and renewals. That means you add your own information into the app. It does not mean we track you around the internet, build an ad profile, or sell your data. That would be the opposite of the point.

If you choose to export a file, back up data, email support, visit this website, or use paid features later, some data may go to the service needed for that action. We explain those cases clearly in the pages below.
